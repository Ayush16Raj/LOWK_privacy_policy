Child Safety Standards — LOWK

Last updated: 09/10/2026

LOWK is an anonymous, proximity-based chat app for adults. Because anonymity can be misused, we hold ourselves to clear, written standards on protecting children from sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). This page describes those standards.

1. Our position

LOWK has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and any form of child sexual abuse or exploitation. Content or behavior of this kind is prohibited on the platform without exception, and we will act on it as described below.

2. Age restriction

LOWK is intended for users 18 and older. We use the age-restriction tools provided by Google Play to prevent minors from installing the app, and our Terms of Use require every user to confirm they are 18 or older before they can use it.

3. Prohibited content and conduct

The following is prohibited in LOWK and in the Terms of Use every user must accept:

  • Any sexual content involving, depicting, or sexualizing minors
  • Soliciting, grooming, or attempting to contact minors for sexual purposes
  • Sharing, requesting, or arranging to trade CSAM in any form
  • Any other content that sexually exploits or endangers a child

4. In-app reporting

Every message in LOWK can be reported directly from the chat screen: tap the sender's name, choose "report this message," and select a reason. Reports are sent to our review system together with the message text and an anonymous per-install identifier, which lets us recognize repeat offenders even if they change the name they use. Reporting is anonymous — we do not collect any information identifying the person who filed a report. Users can also block any sender at any time, which hides all messages from that device on their own phone.

5. How we respond

  • Reports are reviewed by a human, with reports involving possible child safety concerns prioritized above all others.
  • Where content appears to constitute CSAM, we preserve the relevant report data and, as required by applicable law, report it to the appropriate authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline where the law of the relevant jurisdiction requires it, and cooperate with law enforcement requests.
  • We will not knowingly retain or distribute CSAM beyond what is legally required for reporting and investigation.

6. Honest limits of this app's design

LOWK transmits messages directly between nearby phones and does not route them through a server we operate, so we cannot proactively scan or remove messages in transit. Our safeguards are therefore built around age restriction, user-controlled blocking, in-app reporting that reaches our review system, and cooperation with law enforcement. We state this plainly rather than overstate what the technology allows.
