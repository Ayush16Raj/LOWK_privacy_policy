Privacy Policy — LOWK

Last updated: 13/10/2026

LOWK is built around one core idea: you should be able to talk to people physically near you without an account, without a server, and without anyone — including us — reading your messages. This policy explains exactly what that means in practice, and the few places it isn't absolute.

What we don't collect

  • No account. No sign-up. No email, username registration, or password.
  • No phone number is ever requested or collected.
  • Your chat messages are never sent to, or stored on, any server we operate. They travel directly, phone-to-phone, over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, to people physically near you, and are never persisted after the app closes.
  • We do not track your location. We do not log where you've been or build any history of your movements.

Permissions LOWK requests, and why

Android requires these permissions for the peer-to-peer connection technology LOWK uses (Google's Nearby Connections). None of them are used for tracking:

  • Location (Fine/Coarse): required by Android as a prerequisite for Bluetooth and Wi-Fi scanning on many devices. LOWK never reads your GPS coordinates and has no code path that does.
  • Bluetooth (Scan/Advertise/Connect): used to discover and connect to nearby phones running LOWK. Declared with Android's neverForLocation flag, meaning we've told the system we don't use Bluetooth scan results to infer your location.
  • Nearby Wi-Fi Devices / Local Network Access: used the same way, for Wi-Fi-based discovery of nearby phones.

What we do collect: safety reports and feedback

If you tap "report" on a message, LOWK sends the following to our Firebase/Firestore database, the only network request the app ever makes:

  • The alias the reported message was sent under
  • A random, anonymous per-install identifier (see below) tied to the reported device — not yours, unless you're the one being reported
  • The text of the reported message
  • The reason category you selected
  • A timestamp

The chat screen also has a "safety & feedback" button. If you use it, we receive the text you typed and a timestamp, and nothing else.

We deliberately do not collect anything identifying you as the reporter — not your alias, not your device ID, not your IP address. Reporting and feedback are designed to be as anonymous as using the app itself.

The anonymous per-install identifier

LOWK uses Firebase Installations to generate a random identifier unique to your app install, with no personal information attached. It exists for one purpose: letting a repeat offender be recognized across different chosen names, since names in LOWK can be changed freely at any time. This identifier:

  • Is never shown to other users in any readable form
  • Is not linked to your name, phone number, email, or any other personal information
  • Resets completely if you uninstall and reinstall the app

Who can see your messages

Anyone running LOWK within Bluetooth/Wi-Fi range of you can see messages sent to the local mesh, by design — that's how the app works. Messages may also be relayed through other nearby phones to extend their reach, the same way a message passed hand-to-hand would be. Treat LOWK messages the way you'd treat something said out loud in a public room.

Data retention

Chat messages are never stored by us and disappear once the app session ends. Safety reports are retained in our database for as long as needed for safety review and legal compliance. Since there are no accounts, there is no "delete my account" action to offer — if you believe a report was filed about your device and want it removed, contact us at the address below.

Children's privacy

LOWK is intended for users 18 and older and is age-restricted on the Play Store accordingly. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Third-party services

LOWK uses Google Play Services (Nearby Connections) and Google Firebase. Their handling of any data that passes through them is governed by Google's own privacy policy, available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a material way, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, where required, prompt you to review it again in the app.
